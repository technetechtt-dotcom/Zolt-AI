# Privacy notice and POPIA operating policy

Zolt processes account identity/contact data, authentication/security events, administrator actions, tenant configuration, equipment identifiers, telemetry, recommendations and support records to provide, secure, audit and improve the contracted service. Customers remain responsible for lawful collection of plant and personnel data they submit. Access is tenant-scoped and least-privilege; administrator activity is audited.

Retention is tenant-configurable and enforced by archive/deletion jobs. Data-subject access/correction/deletion requests are authenticated, logged, assessed against legal/contractual holds, exported from the tenant export workflow, and completed by an authorised privacy operator. Incidents follow `INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md`. Subprocessors, hosting regions, cross-border safeguards, Information Officer details and regulator/customer contact channels must be completed in the executed customer documentation.
