# Pilot service terms template

Zolt provides advisory analysis only and does not operate plant equipment. Operators remain responsible for engineering review and every operational decision. The customer authorises processing of submitted data, maintains accurate installation configuration, protects its credentials, reports suspected compromise, and does not use the pilot as a safety system.

Availability, support, fees, data ownership/licence, confidentiality, security schedule, retention/deletion, liability, warranties, termination, governing law and POPIA data-processing terms must be completed and signed by authorised representatives. This template is not legal advice and is not an executed agreement.
